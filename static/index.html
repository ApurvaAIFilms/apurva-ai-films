🎬 Apurva AI Films

A private film-development workspace. Choose whether to start from a reference or from your own imagination.

1. Choose your creation mode

Mode A — Reference-BasedUse an authorised video and your instructions to develop a distinct new story.
Mode B — Completely OriginalStart from your own prompt. No reference video needed.

2. Reference material (optional YouTube details + authorised video frames)

A YouTube link alone provides title/thumbnail only. To analyse visuals, upload a video file that you own or are authorised to use. We do not download arbitrary YouTube videos.

2. Describe your completely new movie

No reference video is required. Describe your idea in your own words. You can be brief or detailed.

3. Develop my original movie

The AI will create a development package: title, logline, synopsis, characters, story structure, scene outline and sample dialogue. This step does not render a finished video.

Privacy and cost: Your API key stays in the hosting service's private environment settings. OpenAI API usage is billed separately from ChatGPT. Extracted frames are sent for analysis only when you press the analysis button.
